# M2 — Prices and bills

**Prerequisite:** one or two real, anonymised bills from the user (total incl. VAT, kWh per period,
kVA, option, dates). They become the golden tests of the inverse decomposition. **Ask before
starting.**

**Goal:** price every energy flow correctly, from official dated rates, and explain any bill line
by line. Design: SPEC §3.1, `research/french-household-electricity-bill.md`, decisions 13–15.

## Tasks (to detail when M2 starts)

- Dated rate tables as data (source, effective period, status), French households first: TURPE 7
  ≤ 36 kVA (incl. collective self-consumption options), CTA, excise, VAT, exemptions.
- A scheduled check that reminds when a February/August change is due and not entered.
- Contract (kVA, option, FTA, status, HP/HC hours), seasons and time classes.
- Energy flows: grid import, self-consumption, collective *autoproduite*, export.
- Price books in TOML (bases: `per_kwh`, `per_period`, `tiered_per_kwh`, `percent_of`).
- Forward: itemised bill (layer, payee, flow, period) and marginal value per flow and step.
- Inverse: a real bill → regulated layers + supply remainder, with plausibility checks.
- Notebooks: "anatomy of a bill", "one kWh, four destinations".
