# M2 — Prices and bills

**Goal:** price every energy flow correctly, from official dated rates, and explain any bill line
by line. Design: SPEC §3.1, `research/french-household-electricity-bill.md`, decisions 13–15 and
the M2 decisions below. Rate storage: ADR 0006.

**Prerequisite (done):** the user's real bill is in the git-ignored `private/bills/` (SEC-14). It is
never committed, quoted or published; tests on it run locally only.

## Feasibility check (before planning)

The bill's network lines were rebuilt locally from the official CRE grids (TURPE 6 from 2024-08-01
and 2025-02-01, TURPE 7 from 2025-08-01; CU4, 6 kVA, supplier-held access contract), prorated per
day over the three rate sub-periods. Relative errors only:

| Check | Error |
|---|---|
| CTA = 21.93 % of the TURPE fixed part | −0.01 % |
| TURPE total, season split in proportion to days | −0.72 % |
| TURPE total, mixed periods all high / all low season | +39 % / −30 % |

The fixed part and the dated tables are right; the only real uncertainty is the high/low season
split, because the bill gives HP/HC per sub-period, not per season.

## Decisions (agreed 2026-10-25)

1. **Bills are entered as structured TOML.** No PDF parsing in M2; a `bill_readers` plugin may come
   later.
2. **Supply prices are user input.** Regulated supply tariffs (Tarif Bleu) are left to M9.
3. **Season split:** from a load profile when one is given, otherwise in proportion to days; always
   reported as *estimated*, with its spread.
4. **Tolerances are external parameters** (settings, overridable per call). Defaults: ±1 % on the
   TURPE total, ±0.1 % on CTA, excise and VAT.
5. **Rate-change reminder:** a monthly scheduled CI job fails from mid-January and mid-July when no
   rate version exists for the coming 1 February or 1 August.
6. **Rates are read through a port** (ADR 0006): packaged TOML in M2; a database adapter for the
   M9 agent later, queried by the same keys (component, date, kVA, option, FTA, status).
7. **Metered data later:** an Enedis metered-data source (index per class or load curve) will make
   the time classes exact; the inverse decomposition must accept per-class energies when available
   and only estimate what is missing.

## Tasks

- [x] **Rate tables** — `RateKey`/`RateVersion` domain types (component, validity `[from, to)`,
      selectors: kVA, option, FTA, status; source document; `official` | `to-verify`); the
      `RateSource` port (`catalog`, `history`) and plugin group `myenergy.rate_sources`; a
      packaged-TOML adapter; a missing date or a `to-verify` value used in a bill is an error. Content: TURPE 6 (2024-08, 2025-02) and TURPE 7 (2025-08, 2026-08) for
      BT ≤ 36 kVA (CU, CU4, MU4, MUDT, LU; CG, CC, autoproduction variants), CTA, excise, VAT.
      Golden tests against the CRE deliberations, plus their arithmetic (TURPE 6 multiples of
      0.12 €, TURPE 7 +3.04 % on 2026-08-01). Done: 56 keys, coverage from 2024-08-01;
      MUDT/CU in TURPE 7 are the derogatory options for meters that do not communicate; CACNC is
      left out.
- [ ] **Contract and time classes** — contract (kVA, option Base/HP-HC, FTA, status, access-contract
      holder, off-peak windows); a calendar that turns any `TimeGrid` into HP/HC × season masks on
      the local wall clock.
- [ ] **Energy flows** — grid import, collective import (*autoproduite*), export to grid, export to
      community; on-site self-consumption carries no bill line and is shown as such.
- [ ] **Price books** — TOML, validated at the boundary; bases `per_kwh` (flat, by class, series),
      `per_period` (prorated per day), `tiered_per_kwh`, `percent_of`, `service`; per-line VAT rule
      by date; regulated lines resolved from the rate source.
- [ ] **Forward bill** — flows + contract + price book + period → itemised bill split by rate
      sub-period (layer, payee, flow, period), plus the marginal value per flow and step.
      Properties: a bill is the sum of its lines; proration adds up; marginal value equals a finite
      difference.
- [ ] **Inverse bill** — a TOML bill → regulated layers from the rates, supply remainder, each line
      tagged `stated` | `reconstructed` | `estimated`; checks against the stated TURPE, CTA, excise
      and VAT with configurable tolerances. Local-only golden test on the private bill (skipped
      without `private/`, asserts tolerances, never amounts); a committed synthetic bill with the
      same structure for CI.
- [ ] **Rate-change reminder** — the scheduled job of decision 5.
- [ ] **Notebooks** — `04-anatomy-of-a-bill`, `05-one-kwh-four-destinations`; a script to price a
      period from a profile and a price book.
- [ ] **Docs** — architecture page "prices and bills"; SPEC §3.1 kept in step.

## Out of scope

Tempo/EJP options, contracts above 36 kVA, sharing keys inside a collective operation (community
milestone), PDF parsing, regulated supply tables (M9), metered-data source (roadmap).
